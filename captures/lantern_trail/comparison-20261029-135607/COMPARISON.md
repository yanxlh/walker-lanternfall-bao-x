The student ran the capture command and reported completion. Both variants were rendered on 29 October 2026 from the same preview, real market background, courier scale, 30 fps, and fixed seed 7280. `r0` loads the preserved original effect; `r1` loads the accepted alpha revision. ## Prediction made before editing

Raising the initial/midlife alpha from 0.55/0.30 to 0.95/0.70 should strengthen amber visibility during movement, without changing particle path, count, size, or disappearance time. The transparent endpoint remains zero.
