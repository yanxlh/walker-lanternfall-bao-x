# PROMPTS — Lanternfall: First Delivery

No generation prompts. Nothing in this film was made by an image, video or music model.

- Gameplay: a real Movie Maker capture of tag a7 (`capture/`).
- Captures of earlier assignments: files already committed in this repository.
- Diagrams and boards: drawn by `tools/build_media.py` with Pillow.
- Narration: Kokoro `am_onyx`, run locally, reading the script in `beat_sheet.json`.

The prompts and commands that rebuild the film are in [BUILD-PROMPT.md](BUILD-PROMPT.md).
